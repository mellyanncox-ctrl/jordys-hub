07 me&u — Low Conversion & Next Week's Opportunity

Five me&u attributions this week represents a low conversion rate relative to the list size. The click-through data suggests subscribers are opening the email and not being pulled strongly enough toward an online order. A targeted offer in Campaign 4 would test this directly.

High

Recommended offer for Campaign 4

Suggested Campaign 4 Hook
"This week only — 15% off your online order.
Wednesday to Sunday, subscribers only."
A time-limited discount code dropped into next week's email, linked directly to the me&u order page. One week only — the scarcity is what drives action. At the current average order of $69, a 15% discount costs roughly $10 per order and should more than pay for itself in volume. Set the code to expire Sunday night so it doesn't run indefinitely.
Keep it subscriber-exclusive. The discount should be framed as a reward for being on the list — not a general sale. "As a Jordy's subscriber, here's something just for you this week" lands very differently to a blanket promo. That framing protects the brand and makes the offer feel earned rather than desperate. Test 10% vs 15% — 15% is the threshold where pickup orders tend to meaningfully convert.
