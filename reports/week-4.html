Methodology: Three data sources are cross-referenced for every attribution — Klaviyo open/click records, the NowBookIt run sheet, and me&u order history. A subscriber must appear in Klaviyo with confirmed engagement (open or click) after the 7:00 AM send time, and then appear in either the run sheet or me&u with a booking or order placed after that same time. Bookings made before 7:00 AM on 15 April are excluded regardless of subscriber status. me&u orders are attributed only where the customer's first and last name exactly match an engaged Klaviyo profile — single-first-name orders (e.g. "Olivia", "Daniel") cannot be confirmed and are excluded. Two emails went out this week: Email 1 on Wednesday (3,124 sends across 3 segments, 2,597 opens) and a Friday follow-up (2,319 sends, 67 opens). All 13 attributions traced back to Email 1 — the Friday send drove no confirmed bookings or orders.
