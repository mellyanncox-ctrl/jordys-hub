Growth accelerated in the back half, and it was spend-led. Orders grew at a steady ~19% across both halves, but revenue growth jumped from +23.7% to +36.8% because the average order went from $68.57 to $79.09 in the second window. Something changed around mid-July — menu, pricing, upsell, or the mix of what people ordered. Whatever it was, it is worth identifying and repeating, because it is the highest-yield lever in this report.

Digital penetration has not moved. Roughly one me&u order per three covers, in both years. The extra $13,272 is more people through the door spending slightly more each — not a shift in how they order. That matters for planning: the me&u revenue line is currently a passenger on foot traffic. Orders per cover is the untouched lever, and the only one that scales without another cover walking in.
05 What To Do With This
1
Find out what happened in mid-July
Average order jumped $10.52 between the first and second halves of the period. Check the menu changes, price moves and specials that landed around 11 July. If it was deliberate, do it again in spring. If it was accidental, it is still repeatable.
2
Protect the party-size gain
Bigger tables are carrying the growth. Anything that makes groups of 5+ easy to book — visible group availability, a set menu for 6+, a stated large-group policy — defends the single most valuable trend in this report.
3
Attack me&u orders per cover
Flat at 0.32 for two years running. Table talkers, a second-round prompt, or QR placement per seat rather than per table would move it. Lifting it to 0.40 on 2026 volume is roughly $14,000 of additional revenue over the same two months — without a single extra booking.
4
Decide what the bar is for
Bar covers are down 16% while bar bookings are up 26%. It is being used for pairs. Either that is the plan, or the bar is quietly losing seats on the busiest nights.
06 Method and Caveats
  • Aligned windows. Both years use 11 June – 7 August, which is 34 trading nights in each year. The window ends 7 August because me&u settlement data for 8–10 August 2026 had not yet cleared at the time of reporting.
  • Bookings data comes from the NowBookIt Overview report, all services, all sections, run over identical date ranges in each year.
  • 2025 me&u figures are reconstructed. The me&u Daily Sales report only retains 90 days, so 2025 could not be read directly. It was rebuilt from the payout ledger as payout amount + platform fees + card fees, which reconstructs gross revenue including GST.
  • The reconstruction was validated. Running the same method against 2026 returned $58,058.00 against the me&u portal's own reported revenue of $58,058.00 — an exact match to the cent. The 2025 figure is sound on the same basis.
  • Transactions versus orders. me&u counts 765 transactions and 762 orders for 2026; the gap is split payments on the same order. Transactions are used throughout for consistency with the 2025 payout records.
  • me&u is not total revenue. These figures cover digital ordering only. Anything paid at the POS is excluded, so treat the revenue line as digital-ordering revenue rather than total takings.
