04 Quality of Demand

Volume is only half the picture. The other half is what each booking is worth once it arrives — and on that measure the programme is delivering a better customer, not just more of them.

Every booking is worth more than it was. Parties are bigger and each head spends more, so a reservation that generated $77.89 of digital ordering last winter now generates $89.46 — up 14.9% before a single extra booking is counted. That compounds directly with the volume gain: the marketing is not simply filling more seats, it is filling them with larger, higher-spending groups. Holding the audience where it is and doing nothing else would still leave the venue ahead.
The one flat metric is the opportunity. me&u orders per cover has not moved in two years — roughly one order per three covers, both winters. Digital ordering revenue is currently growing on the back of foot traffic rather than on the back of ordering behaviour. That is the next thing for the programme to attack, and the numbers in section 05 size it.
05 Where We Take It Next
1
Lift me&u orders per cover
The single largest untapped number in this report. Flat at 0.32 across both winters. Moving it to 0.40 on 2026 covers is roughly $14,000 of additional revenue over the same two months — without one extra booking. Levers: in-venue prompts, second-round messaging, QR placement per seat rather than per table, and content that pre-loads what people order before they sit down.
2
Rebuild what happened in mid-July
Average order jumped $10.52 between the first and second halves of the period. We will map the campaigns, specials and menu changes that ran from 11 July against the daily order data, isolate what moved it, and build it into the spring calendar.
3
Defend and extend the group booking gain
Bigger parties are worth 14.9% more per booking. The programme should push harder on the occasions that produce them — group availability made visible at the point of booking, a set menu for 6+, and campaign creative built around tables rather than couples.
4
Finish moving the phone online
Phone is down to 32 bookings across 34 nights. The remaining 5% is the most expensive way the venue takes a reservation. Closing most of that gap is a small piece of work on listings, links and the booking prompts already in market.
5
Turn on campaign-level attribution
This report measures the channels; it does not yet measure the campaigns inside them. The attribution method already used in the monthly campaign reports — matching sends against NowBookIt and me&u — can be run across this whole period so the next one shows revenue by campaign, not just by channel.
One observation for the floor, outside the marketing programme: bar bookings rose from 38 to 48 while bar covers fell from 112 to 94 — average party at the bar dropped from 2.9 to 2.0. Worth a look at how the bar is being allocated on full nights.
06 Method and Caveats
  • Aligned windows. Both years use 11 June – 7 August, which is 34 trading nights in each year. The window ends 7 August because me&u settlement data for 8–10 August 2026 had not yet cleared at the time of reporting.
  • Bookings data comes from the NowBookIt Overview report, all services, all sections, run over identical date ranges in each year. Channel volumes are derived from the reported channel percentages and carry ±1 booking of rounding.
  • Channel, not campaign. This report attributes performance to channel — online, phone, walk-in, me&u. It does not attribute revenue to individual campaigns; that layer is available and is proposed in section 05.
  • 2025 me&u figures are reconstructed. The me&u Daily Sales report only retains 90 days, so 2025 could not be read directly. It was rebuilt from the payout ledger as payout amount + platform fees + card fees, which reconstructs gross revenue including GST.
  • The reconstruction was validated. Running the same method against 2026 returned $58,058.00 against the me&u portal's own reported revenue of $58,058.00 — an exact match to the cent. The 2025 figure is sound on the same basis.
  • Transactions versus orders. me&u counts 765 transactions and 762 orders for 2026; the gap is split payments on the same order. Transactions are used throughout for consistency with the 2025 payout records.
  • me&u is not total revenue. These figures cover digital ordering only. Anything paid at the POS is excluded, so the revenue line is digital-ordering revenue rather than total takings.
