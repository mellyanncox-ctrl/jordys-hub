# Jordy's — brand source of truth

**`JORDYS_SYSTEM.md`** in this folder is the master system doc — the governing document, saved verbatim. It should also live in the project's custom-instructions field so it loads every session; the copy here is the durable record.

The files below are what it refers to at priority 1. Before creating anything customer-facing, read them in this order.

| # | File | What it settles |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **JORDYS_VOICE.md** | How it's written. Hard bans, mechanics, the emphatic-slot rule, before/after. |
| 2 | **APPROVED_COPY.md** | Real approved copy with a "why it works" note on each. Training data, not a swipe file. |
| 3 | **REJECTED_COPY.md** | What was killed and why. The reason is the valuable part. |
| 4 | **BRAND_FACTS.md** | Address, hours, offers, links. Every line sourced and status-marked. |
| 5 | **CURRENT_MENU.md** | Ten pizzas, Groms, calzone, everything else. Verified against the live site. |
| 6 | **SPECIALS.md** | What's on right now, and the rule that a special is furniture not a story. |
| 7 | **CURRENT_PROMOTIONS.md** | The two-format system, the calendar to Christmas, what's live vs draft. |
| 8 | **CHANNELS.md** | Per-channel execution — email, Instagram, stories, web, SMS, signage. |

---

## The two rules that matter most

**1. Never invent an operational fact.** If a menu item, price, date, hour, offer or ingredient isn't in these files with a ✅, ask. A wrong detail in a send to 1,100 people costs more than the email earns. A thinner piece beats a false one.

**2. These files rot.** CURRENT_MENU, SPECIALS and CURRENT_PROMOTIONS are the fast-decaying ones. Each carries a "last verified" date. Check it. Re-fetch `jordyscasuarina.com/menu` before writing anything that cites an item or a count.

---

## Status markers used throughout

✅ verified · ⚠️ needs confirmation · ❌ known wrong, kept as a trap · 🔴 live/urgent

---

## Keeping them alive

When copy is **approved**, add it to APPROVED_COPY.md **with a note on why it works**.
When copy is **rejected for voice reasons**, add it to REJECTED_COPY.md **with the reason**.

Without those notes both files degrade into swipe files, and the voice stops getting more accurate over time — which was the whole point.
