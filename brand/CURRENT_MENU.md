### ⚠ The counting rule — this has shipped wrong once

**Ten pizzas. Ten towns.** Not fifteen.

The **Groms** (kids') and **Kingy** (calzone) are also named after places, but they are **not** part of "the map" and are never counted in the ten. An early draft said *"fifteen pizzas. fifteen towns."* — wrong, and it reached a subject line before it was caught.
