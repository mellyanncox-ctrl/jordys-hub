## What Jordy's is

A premium neighbourhood coastal pizza place. Sourdough. Two-day dough. Ten pizzas named after towns within driving distance.

Not a fine-diner, not a bar with food, not a family restaurant that also does pizza. Playground out the front, so families are real — but the venue doesn't market itself as a kids' venue.
