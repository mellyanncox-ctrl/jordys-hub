## The two-format system

Approved by Mel 2026-07-28. Alternating fortnightly. One format weekly burns out; the alternating slot must be a second *named* format or it regresses to unrelated one-offs.

| | **THE LOCAL** | **THE EXCUSE** |
|---|---|---|
| Slot | Tuesdays 4:30pm, fortnightly | Wednesdays 3:30pm, fortnightly |
| Idea | One pizza, one town | One reason not to cook |
| Points at | The menu | The reader's week |
| Ask | Low — look at this | High — tonight |
| Habit loop | "when's my town?" | "what's this week's excuse?" |
| Runway | 10 towns + 2 bonus ≈ 5 months | Indefinite, but dies the moment it becomes a formula |

Both formats: **one link in the body** (the terracotta "save me a seat" button; the logo also points at booking = 2 hrefs total). Sign-off *"eat your crust, Jordy"*. Same voice across both or they read as two venues.

**The Excuse is harder to write.** It comes off the actual week — rain, last day of term, everyone's sick, nine school runs, the fridge has nothing but condiments, silly-season canapé fatigue. **Specific and true beats clever.** If nobody has a true observation that week, send a town pizza instead and shift the order. That flexibility is the point of having two formats.
